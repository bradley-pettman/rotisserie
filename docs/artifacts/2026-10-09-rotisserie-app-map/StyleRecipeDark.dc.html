Turkey Chili

Prep 15 min · Cook 1 hr
4.35 ratings · made 6 times · last Oct 8
weeknight freezer-friendly + Tag

Ingredients

Recipe makes 4
For 6
1½ lbground turkey 1½onion · diced 3 clovesgarlic · minced 3 tbspchili powder 1½ canskidney beans · 15 oz, drained 1½ canscrushed tomatoes · 28 oz 1½ cupschicken stock

Instructions

1

Brown the turkey with the onion in a large pot, about 8 minutes.

2

Stir in the garlic and chili powder and cook 1 minute.

3

Add the tomatoes, beans and stock. Simmer 45 minutes, stirring now and then.
